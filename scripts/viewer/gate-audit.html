Backtest — 1 · configure → 2 · run → 3 · results → 4 · save as report

A backtest replays a strategy over past bars to estimate how it would have performed. Headline numbers to read first: profit factor, max drawdown and win rate. The split date makes everything after it out-of-sample; the p-value and walk-forward lines say how much to trust the headline. Past results never guarantee future ones.

Optimize — parameter sweep

  
A sweep re-runs the backtest once per combination of settings. Treat the winner as a direction, not a result — picking the best of many runs on one window is how overfitting happens. The objective ranks runs, stability prefers a plateau over a lonely peak, the split date gives an out-of-sample check, and the verdict says whether the sweep found anything at all. Applying a winner writes a decision that later runs can confirm or refute.
